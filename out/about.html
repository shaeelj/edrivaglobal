About Edriva Global

Professional Guidance, Centred on You

Clear advice and practical support for students making important international education decisions.

Who We Are

Edriva Global is an international education consultancy supporting students as they research, prepare and apply for study opportunities abroad.

Our Mission

To make international education planning more transparent, organised and personal for every student we advise.

Our Vision

A world where students can pursue the right education opportunity with confidence and reliable guidance.

Our Approach

We start by listening, then build a practical plan around academic background, priorities, budget and long-term goals.

Our Values

Integrity, transparency, a student-first mindset, global perspective, professional excellence and long-term support guide our work.
